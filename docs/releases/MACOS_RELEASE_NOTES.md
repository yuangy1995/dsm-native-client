## macOS 1.0.17

- 修复升级至 1.0.16 后，有照片权限的账号仍看不到侧栏“照片”和设置中“照片管理”开关的问题。
- 照片入口继续遵循当前账号的照片访问权限；仅有照片权限的账号无需开启文件管理权限。
- 保留现有连接资料、功能开关和照片内容，无需重新设置 NAS 权限。

## English — macOS 1.0.17

- Fixed Photos disappearing from the sidebar and Photo management disappearing from settings after updating to 1.0.16, even when the account has photo access.
- Photos continues to follow the current account's photo permissions. Accounts with photo access do not need file-management permission.
- Existing connections, feature preferences, and photos are preserved. No NAS permission changes are needed.
